Foggy window repair is a sealed unit replacement, not a new window.

A double-pane window is an insulated glass unit: two lites with a sealed, usually argon-filled gap. When the perimeter seal fails, moisture condenses between the panes. That is why foggy window repair in Vancouver is an IGU swap — cleaning never reaches the cavity.

We replace the sealed unit and leave your existing frame in place. Faster and cheaper than full window replacement, and in most Metro Vancouver homes the frame still has years of life left.
