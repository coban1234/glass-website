Glass types for Vancouver repair and replacement

Tempered, laminated, and insulated glass units are different jobs. Guards, showers, and doors carry mandatory safety-glazing rules in BC — we spec the right glass for Metro Vancouver homes and storefronts.

Insulated glass units

Annealed (float)

Standard window glass. Breaks in shards. Not for doors, showers, or guards.

Tempered

Heat-treated safety glass. Breaks into small cubes. Required at many hazardous locations.

Laminated

Two lites with an interlayer. Holds together when broken. Preferred overhead.

Insulated units

Two or more lites with a sealed cavity. The foggy-window product.

Low-E

A coating that reflects heat. Standard on replacement IGUs in this climate.

Argon / krypton

Inert gas fill in the cavity. Lost when the seal fails — defogging does not restore it.

Low-iron

Without the green edge of standard float. Visible on tabletops and thick panels.

Obscure / patterned

Privacy without film. Common in bathrooms and entry sidelights.

Where safety glazing is required

Guards and railings, doors and sidelights, glazing adjacent to tubs, showers and pools, overhead glazing, glass near stairways and landings. Laminated is preferred for overhead and sloped glazing where people are underneath. Requirements depend on the installation. We confirm per project.
